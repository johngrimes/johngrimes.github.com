Selected work

Pathling

2018 – present

Creator and technical lead, CSIRO · pathling.csiro.au

An open-source toolkit for analysing FHIR data with clinical terminology, built on Apache Spark, with libraries for Python, R, Java and Scala.

  • Encodes FHIR data for Spark, and implements FHIRPath and SQL on FHIR views.
  • Runs terminology queries, including SNOMED CT ECL, subsumption and concept translation, inside distributed Spark jobs.
  • Used by research and health organisations including the University of Queensland, SickKids in Toronto, the University of Copenhagen and the Bavarian Cancer Research Center.

SQL on FHIR

2023 – present

Project lead, HL7 International · sql-on-fhir.org

An HL7 specification for portable, tabular views of FHIR data, defined using FHIRPath. A view runs unchanged on any conforming engine, from DuckDB on a laptop to a Spark cluster.

  • Developed through a test and implementation driven process: each change comes with conformance tests, and thirteen independent implementations, including those from Google, Health Samurai, Medplum, NCQA and SAS, report results against them.
  • Most active contributor to the specification, and first author of the paper that describes it in npj Digital Medicine.
  • Took the specification to its September 2026 HL7 ballot, including new operations for running and exporting views and SQL queries, with STU status targeted for 2027.
